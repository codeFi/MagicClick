# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MagicClick to be a friendly place for everyone. We welcome people of every age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, and sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, also with people who are new.
- Respect different opinions and ways of working.
- Give feedback about code and ideas, not about people.
- Accept feedback with grace, and say sorry when you make a mistake.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use MagicClick.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, personal attacks, or mocking comments
- Harassment in public or in private
- Sexual language or images, or unwanted sexual attention
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission
- Trolling, or any other behavior that is not suitable in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any place where you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, contact the maintainer privately at **razvantanase@me.com**. Tell us what happened, where, and when. Add links or screenshots if you can. We read every report and keep the name of the reporter private.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what to do about a report. Depending on how serious it is, the action can be:

1. **A private warning** with an explanation of what was wrong.
2. **A public warning** and a request to change the behavior.
3. **A temporary ban** from the project spaces.
4. **A permanent ban** from the project spaces.
